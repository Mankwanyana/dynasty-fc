Who We Are

About Dynasty FC

Building Young Champions for a Stronger Tomorrow

Our Identity & Mission

Dynasty Football Club is a registered Non-Profit Organisation (NPO) dedicated to youth development and community upliftment through sport. Based in Johannesburg, South Africa, we use football as a vehicle to mentor young athletes, keep youth away from social ills, and build a brighter future for the next generation.

Our programs don't just build footballers — they build confident, disciplined, and community-minded young leaders who carry the values they learn on the pitch into every part of their lives.

Ubuntu is an African expression of togetherness, meaning that what I am is intrinsically linked to what we are.

Our Journey

The Foundation

When Dynasty Founders met, they shared a deep passion for football and a concern about young people growing up in South Africa's toughest communities without mentors or role models. They believed football has the unique power to inspire young people to demand more for themselves — in character, leadership, and education.

These passions became a dream, and in 2016, that dream became a reality. With no sponsors, resources, or even a bank account, Dynasty's first intake of boys was drawn from communities within driving distance. They felt the only way to pursue their dream was to start doing what they could with what they had.

Our Journey Timeline

2016 – The Foundation
Dynasty Football Club is born in Johannesburg with a vision to unite diverse communities through sport.
2017–2020 – Structural Growth
The club achieves official NPO status, standardising operations and establishing solid roots in local community structures.
2021–2024 – Competitive Expansion
Branching out to include dedicated youth development squads and launching a highly competitive women's football division.
2025–2026 – A Regional Force
Cementing our place in SAFA regional frameworks, hosting wide-scale trials, and serving as a vital launchpad for elite talent in Gauteng.

Key Achievements

Registered NPO Status

Fully registered and compliant with the South African Department of Social Development, ensuring structured governance and financial transparency.

Youth Empowerment & Safety

Provided a safe, disciplined, and positive environment for local youth, keeping them actively engaged away from crime and substance abuse.

Grassroots Talent Development

Discovered and nurtured raw community talent, establishing structured training pathways for players to transition into competitive league football.

Competitive League History

Fielded highly competitive, disciplined squads across regional divisions — including our senior structures and the Dynasty Ladies.

Holistic Mentorship

Delivered comprehensive character-building support focused on teamwork, leadership, and personal responsibility beyond the pitch.

Community Trust

Built strong, reliable connections with local families, schools, and community leaders to promote social cohesion and unity.
