About Us

About Dynasty FC

A youth development institution using football to develop disciplined, educated and purpose-driven young people.

Dynasty Football Club

Dynasty Football Club is a youth development institution that uses football as a vehicle to develop disciplined, educated and purpose-driven young people.

Founded in Johannesburg in 2016, the club has spent years building a model that shapes young South Africans not only into skilled footballers, but into individuals of character, integrity and ambition.

Our structured player development system is built on consistency, accountability and long-term growth rather than short-term results. We prioritise clear progression, high standards and an environment where players are developed not just placed.

Our Philosophy

At Dynasty FC, we believe that football is a powerful teacher. On the pitch, young people learn discipline, resilience, decision-making and how to perform under pressure.

Off the pitch, those same qualities shape how they approach their studies, their relationships and their futures. Our philosophy is simple: develop the whole person.

Football is the vehicle. Purpose is the destination.

Our Objective

Our objective is to develop complete players and complete people. On the pitch, we develop footballers who can read the game, perform under pressure, and play with confidence. Off the pitch, we develop individuals who can navigate life with purpose and a strong foundation of values.

We aim to provide a platform for aspiring footballers to develop their skills, knowledge, and character, enabling them to reach their full potential on and off the field.

Winning is not the ultimate goal. Growth is.

Our Mission

Who we are and what we do

Dynasty Football Club is committed to becoming a force in youth development and coaching in South Africa. Our foundation provides our players and coaches with personal attention and excellence. We also empower community-based projects by assisting them with coaching and development.

We aim to provide a platform for aspiring footballers to develop their skills, knowledge, and character, enabling them to reach their full potential on and off the field.

Our Vision

What we aspire to be

To be one of South Africa's leading youth development institutions, using football as a platform to produce not only talented players, but disciplined, educated and purpose-driven young people who make a lasting impact in their communities and on the world stage.

Our Core Values

History of the Club

2016

2016

Dynasty Football Club is born in Johannesburg with a vision to unite diverse communities through sport. The first intake of boys was drawn from communities within driving distance.

2017

2017

The club achieves official NPO status, standardising operations and establishing solid roots within local community structures.

2021

2021

Branching out to include dedicated youth development squads across Under 10 and Under 15 age groups.

2024

2024

Launch of a highly competitive women's football division, the Diamond Divas, expanding our impact and reach across Gauteng.

2025

2025

Cementing our place in SAFA regional frameworks, hosting wide-scale trials and serving as a launchpad for elite young talent in Gauteng.

2026

2026

Dynasty FC continues to grow as a regional force, with over 44 registered players across four competitive teams and a growing community of supporters.
